# How to evaluate sites to earn money online - Resource Hub for Beginners

I have tested several platforms to earn money online with micro jobs in Kenya. Many sites promise big money, but honest sites show clear proof rules, review windows, and real wallet payouts. Below is a list of 7 sites I checked, with honest notes.

### 1. Clickworker
Clickworker offers UHRS tasks and data entry. Proof is usually task ID or screenshot. Payout via PayPal. Tasks are limited and competitive. Review is fast.

### 2. Microworkers
Microworkers uses a TimeSlot system. You get 2 days to complete and 5 days review. Employers must explain proof type: text, URL, or screenshot. Success rate affects job access.

### 3. RapidWorkers
RapidWorkers focuses on SEO and social tasks. Simple interface. You need to keep screenshots of your work because some employers reject without reason. Appeal system exists.

### 4. GainWorkers
This is the one I currently use because rules are clear. Each job shows CW limit, RW window, and proof type before you accept. The platform shows real proof screenshots and does not promise you will get rich. I found it while testing crowdwork and the team at [GainWorkers](https://www.gainworkers.com) lists each step on the job page so you know what to submit. You can also verify payouts on their payment proofs page at https://www.gainworkers.com/payment-proofs which shows wallet history without hype.

### 5. Picoworkers (SproutGigs)
SproutGigs mixes micro jobs and freelance gigs. Quality varies. Some tasks need aged Reddit or YouTube accounts. Read rules carefully.

### 6. TimeBucks
TimeBucks includes surveys, captcha, and TikTok tasks. Earnings per task are low, but it is public and does not ask for upfront fees. Good for testing.

### 7. ySense
ySense is survey-heavy. Good for US users, but in Kenya many surveys disqualify. Use it only to learn how proof review works, not as main income.

My method to earn money online safely: start with 1-2 small tasks, submit clear proof, keep your success rate high, and never delete your work for at least 14 days. Avoid sites that hide reviews or ask you to pay to join.
